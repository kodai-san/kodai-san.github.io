Oxford STRIDE

A student humanoid robotics team at the Oxford Robotics Club.

Overview

Since October 2026 I've been a project lead of Oxford STRIDE, a student team in the Oxford Robotics Club.

  • Co-leading the team building the Berkeley Humanoid Lite, to conduct research on locomotion and loco-manipulation.
  • Supervising the RoboCup 3D simulation team, currently training baseline policies for skills such as passing and kicking using PPO and AMP in MuJoCo.
