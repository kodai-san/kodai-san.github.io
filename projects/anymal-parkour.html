ANYmal D Sparse-Terrain Parkour

Teaching a quadruped to cross stepping stones with reinforcement learning.

Overview

For my fourth-year project at Oxford, supervised by Ioannis Havoutis, I'm training an ANYmal D to cross sparse terrain like stepping stones, where one misplaced foot means a fall. The policy does this well with a perfect height map, so my focus now is making it work on the less accurate maps built from depth cameras.

All of this work is in simulation so far, and these policies haven't been run on a real robot. The project builds on Benjy McCartney's 2025-26 project, whose policy ran on a real ANYmal. Thanks also to Jacques Cloete for the pipeline extension and cluster template, and to Vassil Atanassov and Matias for robot and deployment support.
