Unitree Go2-W Wheeled-Legged Parkour

Moving the parkour pipeline onto a wheeled quadruped.

Overview

After the ANYmal D work, I moved the same training pipeline onto the Unitree Go2-W, a much smaller quadruped with wheels for feet. The policy controls both the legs and the wheels, and trains across 15 terrain types.

This work is in simulation, and the policy hasn't been run on a real robot. The pipeline builds on work by Benjy McCartney and Jacques Cloete, and the robot model comes from robot_lab.
