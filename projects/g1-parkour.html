Unitree G1 Humanoid Parkour

Applying the same parkour pipeline to a humanoid.

Overview

The third robot in this series swaps four legs for two. I trained a policy for the Unitree G1 humanoid that controls its 12 leg joints across terrain like steps, gaps, slopes and poles, then tested how well it recovers from pushes.

This work is in simulation, and the policy hasn't been run on a real robot. The pipeline builds on work by Benjy McCartney and Jacques Cloete, and the robot model comes from unitree_ros.
