Small bipedal robot

Electronics, calibration and interaction policies at Algomatic, Tokyo.

Overview

I spent March and April 2026 as an AI robotics engineering intern at Algomatic, a robotics start-up in Tokyo, working on the electronics and software of a novel small bipedal robot.

  • Improved the imitation-learning policy and raised the walking success rate on the Open Duck robot by 80 %, with a new calibration pipeline and custom PCBs for better sensor–microcontroller integration.
  • Developed a human-interaction policy in MuJoCo and deployed it with capacitive touch sensors and infrared proximity sensors.
  • Designed the power management system for the new robot, supporting 2 CyberGears and a Jetson Orin AGX.
